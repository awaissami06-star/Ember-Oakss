OUR STORY

Started in a garage roaster, still run the same stubborn way.

Two friends, one roaster, no investors.

Ember & Oak started in 2016 as a 1kg sample roaster in a rented garage. We were tired of coffee that prioritized speed over the farmer who grew it, so we built the opposite: slower sourcing, smaller batches, and a bar staff that's required to taste everything before it goes on the menu.

A decade later we're still doing it the same stubborn way — just with better equipment and three doors instead of one.
